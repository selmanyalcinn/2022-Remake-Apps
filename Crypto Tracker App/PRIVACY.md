# Cryptic Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Cryptic is a read-only cryptocurrency market tracker. It does not create user accounts, connect to wallets, execute trades, request private keys, or sell personal information.

## Information handled by the app

- Favorites, theme, language, and currency preferences are stored locally on the user's device.
- The app requests public market and chart data through Cryptic's API proxy. The hosting provider may temporarily process standard technical request information such as IP address, timestamp, and device/network metadata for security, delivery, and operational logs.
- Cryptic does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, payment information, wallet credentials, or advertising identifiers.

## Third-party services

Public cryptocurrency market data is supplied by CoinGecko. API delivery is hosted on Vercel. Their own privacy practices may apply to technical traffic processed by their services.

## Data retention and deletion

Preferences remain on the device until the user changes them, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Cryptic does not maintain a user profile or cloud portfolio to delete.

## Children

Cryptic is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. Questions can be sent through the support contact shown on Cryptic's official store listing.
