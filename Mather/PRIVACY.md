# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Mather is designed to work offline and does not require an account.

## Data stored on your device

Mather stores game progress, preferences, daily completion state, and statistics locally through AsyncStorage. This information is not uploaded by the app.

## Data we do not collect

Mather does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, user accounts, or a custom backend. It does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or payment details.

## Sharing

When you choose to share a daily result, Mather opens the operating system's share sheet with an emoji-only result summary. Nothing is shared until you select a destination. The hidden equation is not included.

## Haptic feedback

Haptic feedback uses the device vibration system and can be disabled in Settings.

## Deleting data

Use **Settings → Reset game data** to remove saved games and statistics. Preferences are retained. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data according to the operating system's behavior.

## Contact

Until a public support address is added, privacy questions can be opened as an issue in this project's GitHub repository.
